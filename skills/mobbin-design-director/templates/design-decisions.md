# Design decisions

Locked, owner-approved design decisions. Agents read this before
proposing anything. Locked decisions are constraints, and rejected
patterns stay rejected.

Rules:
- Only decisions the owner approved: one line each, dated, with the
  evidence or reasoning that grounds it.
- Choices as evidence of taste go in `design-taste.md`. Research goes in
  `docs/research/`.
- To change a decision, edit its line in place and say what replaced it
  and why.

## Decided
- <YYYY-MM-DD> <decision> (<evidence / rationale / packet>)

## Defaults to review (picked by the agent, not the owner)
Small details the agent chose under a platform rule or convention, so the
owner can review them when they want. Not decisions until the owner
confirms them.
- <YYYY-MM-DD> <what was picked> (<rule, evidence, or labeled hypothesis>)

## Undecided (don't treat as settled)
- <question> (see <packet>)

## Rejected (don't bring back)
- <YYYY-MM-DD> <pattern>
