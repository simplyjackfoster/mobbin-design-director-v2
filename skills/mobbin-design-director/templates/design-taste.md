# Design taste

The owner's visual preferences, **as shown by actual choices**. They narrow
which options get shown. They do not establish usability. Revisit research when an open decision needs it;
do not manufacture alternatives to an already explicit owner choice.

Confidence:
- **one-off:** a single choice
- **emerging:** two consistent choices in comparable situations
- **established:** three or more, or an explicit "always"/"never" from the owner

Preferences can reverse, so date everything. Record choices, not
adjectives: "chose flat sections over nested cards on X", never "likes
premium".
