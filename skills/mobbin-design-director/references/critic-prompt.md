# Independent critic prompt

Use a fresh context when the environment supports it. Include only relevant raw
artifacts and constraints, not the implementer's desired conclusion. This template
supports interaction and visual review; select the lenses justified by the task.

---

Review this experience. Do not redesign or modify files unless explicitly asked.

**Audience/context:** <known evidence and separately labeled assumptions>
**Surface/target:** <platform, supported configuration>
**Approved constraints:** <relevant product decisions and taste context>
**Artifacts:** <runnable surface, flow, screenshots, recording, test evidence>
**Known limits:** <fixtures, missing branches/tools, unavailable user research>
**Review lenses:** <interaction, discovery, recovery, accessibility, visual craft>

**Stage 1:** Record your independent assessment from the task and artifacts before
reading the implementer's applicability checklist or generated acceptance criteria.
User-approved requirements belong in the initial constraints. Do not request the
implementer's persuasive rationale.

Open the supplied images. Exercise the task if the surface and permissions allow
it; otherwise distinguish observation, source inference, and hypothesis. A screenshot
cannot prove state persistence, latency, assistive behavior, or unseen transitions.

For first-use concerns, check whether the assumed user can identify the goal/action,
notice the action, associate it with the goal, and recognize progress. For visual
craft inspect hierarchy, grouping, typography, rhythm, character, and coherence
using real content at actual scale. Preserve successful behavior.

Prioritize consequential findings; no minimum count. For each give:
- Concrete fact and affected task/state, with artifact location.
- Why it matters and the applicable criterion or source rationale.
- Kind: verified defect / usability risk / aesthetic preference.
- Severity: critical / major / minor, based on consequences.
- Confidence: high / medium / low, with reason and untested assumptions.
- Fix direction, and the check that would confirm improvement.

**Stage 2:** After saving the initial findings, compare the implementer's checklist
and acceptance criteria with your assessment. Identify missing areas or scenarios,
wrong exclusions, unjustified departures, and evidence whose method cannot support
the claim. Check that current artifacts match the evaluated version. A hash or
validator pass establishes neither usability nor evidence truth. Distinguish a
checklist omission from an observed product defect; add concrete missing criteria.

End with strengths to preserve, the most important next action, and verification
limits. Do not call agent inspection a participant study or fabricate evidence.
