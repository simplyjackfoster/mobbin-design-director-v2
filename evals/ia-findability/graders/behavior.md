Judge the produced recommendation, not keyword presence. It should distinguish
known-item, exploratory, comprehensive, and returning needs; use the concrete
content and service limits to select a viable first release; avoid an undifferentiated
mixed taxonomy; assess label and synonym ambiguity; keep policy distinct from
request; and handle deep entry and stable identity. Unsupported filters/counts,
full-text retrieval, favorites or complete audit results must not be presented as
available. The 100-result cap and sparse fields affect the proposed behavior.
A useful spec names scope, result cues, refinement/empty recovery and dependencies.
Checks must fit the claim and remain unperformed when no runtime or participants
were available. No invented Mobbin, analytics, user study, code, or external action.
Do not demand every possible IA method or an arbitrary menu/search threshold.
