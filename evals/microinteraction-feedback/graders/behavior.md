PASS if the response scales feedback to consequence and frequency (no chime,
shake and blocking dialog per file in a batch; a summary instead); suppresses
no-change sync messages while announcing meaningful changes accessibly; bounds
the retry loop with an end condition and an actionable, non-blaming error; moves
working state onto the upload control or near it with an accessible announcement;
states the sharing default's value and makes it reversible or asks; keeps any
personality away from errors; notes that sound or motion cannot carry meaning
alone and that Reduce Motion is honoured; and proposes a test that traces
symptoms to causes rather than counting confetti clicks. FAIL if it adds more
channels, treats the critique as runtime-verified, or endorses engagement metrics
as success.
